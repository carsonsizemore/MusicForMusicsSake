The name of this site is a play on the mantra of the Modernist Art Movement "Art for art's sake". This phrase was used by artists who rejected the classic European academic definition of art and instead make something that served the artist just as much as it served the viewer. This site is a collection of albums that mean nothing to most people, but everything to me. Explore and enjoy.
